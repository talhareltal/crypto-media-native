# Contributing

PRs welcome. Same rules as the skill: short, direct, no fluff.

## Hard rules

- **No real company names in examples.** Invent them. PRs that name a real company or real release get sent back.
- **No naming outlets as pay-to-play.** Describe the signal, not the outlet.
- **No hype words.** If it's banned in `fluff-patterns.md`, it's banned here.
- **No em dashes.**
- **Every new pattern needs a fix.** Don't just say what's wrong. Say what to do instead.

## Good contributions

- A new announcement type with news-if / not-news-if / have-ready
- A fluff pattern you see every week
- A teardown example (invented names)
- A failure autopsy: a bad AI answer and why it's bad

## Reporters

If you cover crypto and think something here is wrong, open an issue. You're the people this is calibrated against.
