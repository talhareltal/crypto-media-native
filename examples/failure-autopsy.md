# Failure autopsy

What a bad answer looks like, and why. All names invented.

## The ask

"We hit 2 million wallets on Kestrel Finance. Is this worth a press release?"

## The bad answer

> Congratulations on this impressive milestone! 2 million wallets is a significant achievement that demonstrates strong community growth and product-market fit. This could make a great press release. Here are 8 tips for writing an effective milestone announcement:
> 1. Lead with the number...

## What went wrong

1. **Opened with praise.** Wasted the first line.
2. **Took the number at face value.** Didn't ask what a wallet means here.
3. **Called it product-market fit.** No evidence for that.
4. **Said yes without the test.** A milestone with no definition fails "proof".
5. **Gave eight tips.** Nobody asked for tips. Too long.

## The right answer

**Verdict:** Borderline.

**Why:** 2M wallets is big if they're real users. Reporters will assume airdrop farming until you show otherwise.

**Missing:** How many are active monthly. What share came from incentive campaigns. A public dashboard to check it.

**Do this:** If monthly active users are strong, lead with that number instead. If most wallets are farmers, skip the release and post it on X.

**A reporter will ask:** How many of these wallets have transacted in the last 30 days? What happens when the incentives end?
