# Teardown: a partnership release

All names invented.

## The input

> **Halcyon Protocol Announces Strategic Partnership with Driftwood Labs to Accelerate Web3 Adoption**
>
> Halcyon Protocol, a leading next-generation Layer 2 solution, today announced a strategic partnership with Driftwood Labs, a premier blockchain infrastructure provider. The collaboration will explore opportunities to bring seamless cross-chain experiences to millions of users.
>
> "We are thrilled to partner with Driftwood," said the CEO of Halcyon. "Together we will unlock the next wave of mass adoption."

## The output

**Verdict:** Not news.

**Why:** Nothing happened. "Will explore" is a plan, not a partnership. No product, no money, no date.

**Missing:** What users can do now. Whether Driftwood announced it too. A launch date. Any number.

**Do this:** Don't send it to media. Post a joint X thread from both accounts. Come back with a release when the integration is live and you have first usage numbers.

## Why this output is right

- Verdict first, no warm-up.
- Under 80 words.
- Names the exact failure ("will explore").
- Gives a better channel, not just a no.
- Doesn't lecture about the adjectives. They're not the main problem.
