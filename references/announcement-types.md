# Announcement types

The standard crypto news moments. For each: when it's news, when it isn't, what to have ready.

## Funding round

**News if:** amount disclosed, lead investor named, round stage clear. Bigger is easier, but a small round with a notable lead or a contrarian thesis works too.
**Not news if:** "strategic investment" with no number. Undisclosed amount from unnamed backers.
**Weak if:** no lead named. Reporters notice. If it was co-led, say so.
**Have ready:** amount, lead, participants, valuation if you'll share it, what the money is for, founder and lead investor available for quotes.
**Note:** funding news is crowded. Exclusives work well here.

## Token launch / TGE

**News if:** it's a meaningful project with real usage, a notable listing, or an unusual distribution model.
**Not news if:** it's just a token existing now.
**Have ready:** tokenomics, unlock schedule, what the token does, where it trades.
**Careful:** anything that reads like price promotion. Loop in counsel. Reporters will ask about insiders and unlocks. Have honest answers.

## Exchange listing

**News if:** a top-tier exchange, or a first listing in a major market.
**Not news if:** a small exchange. Most listings are not news.
**Have ready:** date, pairs, the exchange confirming it.

## Mainnet / product launch

**News if:** it's live and people can use it today.
**Not news if:** testnet, beta, waitlist, "coming soon".
**Have ready:** live link, what's new vs. competitors, early usage numbers if real, demo.

## Partnership / integration

**News if:** money moves, users get something new, both sides announce, and the partner is someone people know.
**Not news if:** MOU, "strategic collaboration", "exploring", integration that's an API key or a logo on a page.
**Have ready:** partner quote, what users can do now that they couldn't before, date it goes live.

## Exec hire

**News if:** the person is a recognisable name, or the hire signals a strategy shift (ex-regulator, ex-TradFi exec).
**Not news if:** a good hire nobody outside the industry has heard of.
**Have ready:** why this person, why now, what changes.

## Milestones and metrics

**News if:** defined, verifiable, and meaningful against competitors.
**Not news if:** wallets, users or TVL with no definition, or driven by incentives and airdrop farming.
**Have ready:** methodology, a source others can check (dashboard, on-chain query).
**Reporters will ask:** how many are real users? What happens when incentives end?

## Hack or exploit

This is not an announcement. It's damage control.
**Priority:** give clarity as fast as you can. Acknowledge it, say what you know, say what users should do, say when the next update is.
**Don't:** speculate on cause, promise full recovery you can't guarantee, go silent, blame users.
**Have ready:** a holding statement template before you ever need it.
**Always:** loop in counsel and security partners before naming causes or amounts.

## Regulatory / license

**News if:** a meaningful license in a major market, or a first for the category.
**Have ready:** exactly what the license allows. Don't overstate it.
**Careful:** reporters will check the regulator's register. Make sure it's there.

## Research or data report

**News if:** original data, a clear finding, and it says something about the market, not just about you.
**Not news if:** a survey of 200 people concluding your product is needed.
**Have ready:** methodology, raw numbers, one chart that tells the story.
**Note:** often the best route to coverage for companies with no hard news.

## Rebrand

Almost never news. Unless the rebrand is the result of something that is news (merger, pivot, legal issue).
