# The news test

Run this on anything before it becomes a press release.

## Four checks

**1. Change.** Did something happen? Money moved, product is live, contract signed, number confirmed. "Plans to", "will", "exploring" and "set to" fail this check.

**2. Proof.** Can an outsider confirm it? On-chain data, a live link, a filing, the other party announcing it too. "Trust us" fails.

**3. Specifics.** Named parties, real amounts, dates, defined metrics. "A leading exchange" and "significant growth" fail.

**4. Stakes.** Does anyone outside the company care? Users, traders, investors, regulators, competitors. If only your team and your investors care, it fails.

Fail two or more: not news.
Fail one: borderline. Fix that one thing first.
Pass all four: news. Now it's about the angle and the timing.

## Borderline: how to push it over

- Add the number you were hiding.
- Wait until it's live.
- Get the partner to co-announce.
- Bundle three small updates into one real one.
- Find the trend it proves. Your launch is not the story. The shift it represents might be.
- Add data nobody else has.

## Not news: where it goes instead

| It's... | Put it on |
|---|---|
| A product update for existing users | Blog, changelog, community channels |
| A minor partnership or integration | X thread, both teams post it |
| A hire below C-level | LinkedIn |
| An award or ranking | Social, website, sales deck |
| A conference appearance | Social, before and after |
| A roadmap or vision | Founder op-ed or long X post |

Not news doesn't mean worthless. It means wrong channel.

## Already published

If the release is already out (wire, blog, X), don't grade it like a draft. Say:
- what it got right
- what a reporter would still need to cover it
- the follow-up angle that could still earn coverage (data, a customer story, a trend piece)

## Quarter planning

Given a list of planned updates, sort into:

- **Release-worthy:** passes the test on its own
- **Bundle:** weak alone, stronger together
- **Social only:** real but small
- **Hold:** not true yet

Return a table. One line of reasoning per item. Flag anything that clashes on timing.
