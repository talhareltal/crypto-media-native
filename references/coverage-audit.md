# Coverage audit

For when someone says "we got 80 placements". Most of them probably aren't coverage.

## Sort every placement into one of five

| Type | What it is | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Earned** | A reporter chose to write about you. Independent byline, other sources, their own framing. | High |
| **Contributed** | Op-ed or guest post with your name on it. | Medium |
| **Mention** | You're quoted or named in someone else's story. | Medium, depends on context |
| **Sponsored** | Paid content. Usually labelled sponsored, partner, promoted or press release. | Low |
| **Syndicated** | Your press release republished, often word for word, via a wire service or paid distribution. | Very low |

## How to tell

- **Search one distinctive sentence** from the release. If it shows up on 50 sites word for word, that's syndication.
- **Check the label.** Look at the top and bottom of the article for "press release", "sponsored", "partner content" or a disclaimer.
- **Check the byline.** No byline, "staff", or "press release" means not earned.
- **Check for other voices.** Earned stories usually quote someone outside your company.
- **Check the framing.** If the headline is your headline, it's probably your release.

## Metrics that mean nothing

- "Potential reach" or "audience of X million" (counts the outlet's total traffic, not who saw your piece)
- Advertising value equivalent
- Number of placements without the breakdown above
- Syndication to finance portals presented as coverage on those portals

## Metrics that mean something

- Number of earned stories, by outlet
- Did reporters reach out afterward?
- Traffic, signups or inbound from specific pieces
- Did the story get cited by others?

## Questions to ask your agency

1. How many of these were earned?
2. Which reporters did you pitch, and who said no?
3. Which placements were paid, and how much?
4. What came out of it? Inbound, traffic, follow-ups?

A good agency answers these without blinking.

## Rules

- Don't accuse a named outlet of selling coverage unless the content is labelled or it's publicly documented.
- Syndication isn't evil. It's just not coverage. Pay for it knowing what it is.
- Your own agency is not exempt. Run this on everyone, including whoever wrote this skill.
